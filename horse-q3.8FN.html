How this is made

No model file, no animation clip, no build step — the horse is sculpted from boxes, prisms and icosahedra, and every frame is solved from a single cycle parameter.

this panel
The gait engine places each hoof on a stride path locked to ground speed, then solves a three-bone limb analytically — so the feet never slip. Body, spine, neck, head, mane and tail are all periodic functions of one phase, which is why the loop has no seam; the readout on the right measures that seam every stride.
